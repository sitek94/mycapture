# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-15

- Crisp icons: filled, heavier glyphs of one size in the editor toolbar, its bottom bar and the thumbnail; the Arrow tool shows ↙ and the color button a dot in a white ring. The Drag Me border is even all around on non-Retina screens.
- ⌃⇧⌘H opens Capture History; Restore Last Capture has no default shortcut now (set one in Settings → Shortcuts).
- Capture History (⌃⇧⌘H or the menu): the last 3 days of captures in a strip at the top of the screen. Restore one as a thumbnail, Quick Look, copy, annotate, drag out, remove. Keep History and Clear History in Settings → History.
- Settings in a sidebar as in System Settings, with colored tiles: General, Shortcuts, History, Permissions, About. Shortcuts show as key fields.
- A new look: the thumbnail has rounder corners, dark glass on hover and light frosted Copy, Save, Close and Annotate buttons; Copy and Save show a checkmark when they worked. Capture History shows each capture with the icon of its app, the time under it, an accent ring and Restore on the selected one; the pointer selects a card.
- Annotation editor, first tool: arrows. Double-click a thumbnail, click its pencil button or choose Annotate in its menu. Draw, select, move, restyle (color, line width) and delete arrows, with undo. Arrows stay editable when you open the capture again.
- Double-click on a thumbnail opens the editor instead of Preview.
- Editor layout: tools and the current tool's color and width in the title bar, Save As… on the right (Save with ⌥ held: to the screenshots folder); below the image, Drag Me (drag the file out; a click closes) and Copy. Move tool (V). ⌘ Q closes the editor only.
- Closing the editor keeps your changes in the capture and asks nothing; they stay editable.
- Controls use the system accent color (System Settings → Appearance), not a myCapture color.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-13

First version.

- Area and window captures with macOS's selection tool, to a floating thumbnail or to the clipboard.
- The thumbnail: drag out, Copy, Save, Save As, Quick Look, Preview, hover keys, a right-click menu. Restore brings back the last closed one; thumbnails on screen come back after a relaunch.
- Optional screen freeze; a crosshair and magnifier while ⌘ is held.
- File names with the date, app and window title.
- Settings: shortcuts, freeze, sound, launch at login, screenshots folder, permissions.
