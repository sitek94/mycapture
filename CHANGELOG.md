# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-13

First version.

- Area and window captures with macOS's selection tool, to a floating thumbnail or to the clipboard.
- The thumbnail: drag out, Copy, Save, Save As, Quick Look, Preview, hover keys, a right-click menu. Restore brings back the last closed one; thumbnails on screen come back after a relaunch.
- Optional screen freeze; a crosshair and magnifier while ⌘ is held.
- File names with the date, app and window title.
- Settings: shortcuts, freeze, sound, launch at login, screenshots folder, permissions.
